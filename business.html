The partnership vision

A real role during the school day — designed by you.

Selected students come on-site Monday through Thursday for about a 2.5-hour block (schedule open to adjust). They work the role you design. Students handle their own transportation.

  • You design the intern. Duties, skill bar, duration, and how many seats you’ll hold.
  • Students apply with proof. Applications plus CMYK skills credentials with evidence.
  • You decide who to accept. CMYK prepares applicants; your team makes the call.
  • Agreements protect both sides. CMYK provides a student/family/business contract on safety and supervision. You’re welcome to add your own.
