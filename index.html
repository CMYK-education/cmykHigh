The CMYK model

School, rebuilt around the student.

We kept the parts of high school that change a life and rebuilt the rest — four elements that layer together like process inks into a full-color education.

Focused, personalized academics

Best-in-class AI tutors deliver core academics one-on-one, adapting to each student's pace and gaps — including fast credit recovery and an accelerated path for those ready to move. Master more in two focused hours than a whole day of lectures.

Human skills, taught on purpose

Communication, critical thinking, collaboration, self-management, and character. These aren't extracurricular here — they're core curriculum, practiced daily in real projects and discussion.

Mentorship + CTE pathways

Every student has a dedicated coach who knows them and holds a high bar — plus a real career direction. CTE pathways connect learning to skilled trades, tech, design, and business through hands-on work.

Outdoor experiential learning

The Wasatch is our second campus. Weekly expeditions, service, and hands-on challenges build resilience, teamwork, and a body that's as engaged as the mind.

Career & technical education

A real direction — not just a diploma.

Every student explores CTE pathways that turn "why does this matter?" into "I'm building something." Real skills, real credentials, real momentum toward work worth doing.

Design & digital media

Graphic design, video, and creative tools — a natural fit for visual, creative thinkers.

Technology & making

Coding, AI, robotics, and hands-on fabrication — building things that actually work.

Skilled trades & business

Entrepreneurship, construction, and the trades — pathways to independence and real earning.

Internships & certifications

Work-based learning and industry credentials that mean something after graduation.

Taught by AI · coached by humans

The best of both — nothing lost between them.

AI does what software does best: infinite patience, instant feedback, a perfectly personalized path. Our people do what only people can: believe in a kid, push them, and know them.

Questions, answered

The things families ask first.

Why "CMYK"?

CMYK is the four inks — cyan, magenta, yellow, and black — that layer together to make every color you've ever seen. It's how we see our students: each one distinct, each essential, and capable of far more in combination than any single track allows. It also nods to the creative, hands-on, design-and-make spirit of our CTE pathways.

Who is CMYK High the right fit for?

Capable teens who've become bored, disengaged, or lost in a traditional classroom — including school refusal, lost confidence from an earlier rough experience, kids who need credit recovery, kids ready to accelerate, and bright students who simply learn differently. If your student is capable but "checked out," this was built for them.

Where is the school located?

CMYK High is based in Lehi, Utah, on the Wasatch Front — with the mountains, canyons, and rivers of northern Utah serving as our outdoor campus for weekly field days and expeditions.

What are CTE pathways?

Career & Technical Education pathways connect academics to real careers — design and digital media, technology and making, skilled trades and business — through hands-on projects, internships, and industry credentials. They give students a reason for the work and a head start on a future.

How big are the cohorts?

Small by design. We keep cohorts intentionally limited so every student is truly known by their coach and mentors. Our founding class is especially small — one reason to join the waitlist early.

What about accreditation, tuition, and a diploma?

We're finalizing these details as we prepare to open, and accreditation and a recognized path to graduation are core priorities. Waitlist families will be the first to receive full information on tuition, the academic pathway, and enrollment.
