The Lodestar model

School, rebuilt around the student.

We stripped away the parts of high school that waste a teenager's time and kept the parts that change a life — then rebuilt the rest.

Focused, personalized academics

Best-in-class AI tutors deliver core academics one-on-one, adapting to each student's pace and gaps. Master more in two focused hours than a whole day of lectures — then spend the rest of the day doing.

Human skills, taught on purpose

Communication, critical thinking, collaboration, self-management, and character. These aren't extracurricular here — they're the core curriculum, practiced daily in real projects and discussion.

Coaches and mentorship

Every student has a dedicated coach who knows them, tracks their growth, and holds them to a high bar. Live teaching classes and adult mentors turn potential into momentum.

Outdoor experiential learning

The Wasatch is our second campus. Weekly expeditions, service, and hands-on challenges build resilience, teamwork, and a body that's as engaged as the mind.

Taught by AI · coached by humans

The best of both — nothing lost between them.

AI does what software does best: infinite patience, instant feedback, a perfectly personalized path. Our people do what only people can: believe in a kid, push them, and know them.

Questions, answered

The things families ask first.

Is this really "taught by AI"?

Core academics are delivered one-on-one by best-in-class AI tutors, which lets every student learn at their own pace and reach true mastery. But learning is coached, guided, and mentored by real adults every single day. The AI handles instruction; humans handle the human — motivation, discussion, character, and relationship.

Who is Lodestar the right fit for?

Capable teens who have become bored, disengaged, or lost in a traditional classroom — kids who could do more than their current school is asking of them. If your student is bright but "checked out," this was built for them.

Where is the school located?

Lodestar Academy is based in Lehi, Utah, on the Wasatch Front — with the mountains, canyons, and rivers of northern Utah serving as our outdoor campus for weekly field days and expeditions.

How big are the cohorts?

Small by design. We keep cohorts intentionally limited so every student is truly known by their coach and mentors. Our founding class is especially small — one reason to join the waitlist early.

What about accreditation, tuition, and a diploma?

We're finalizing these details as we prepare to open, and accreditation and a recognized path to graduation are core priorities. Waitlist families will be the first to receive full information on tuition, the academic pathway, and enrollment.
