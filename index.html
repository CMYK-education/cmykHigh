The problem we're solving

Capable teens are checking out.

Bright kids can do the work — and still feel like none of it connects to a real person or a real future. Scores show an outcome. They rarely show purpose, growth, or the full student.

“Why am I doing this?”

That question shows up over worksheets, revisions, drills, and lectures. Left unanswered, it turns capable teens into students who are just going through the motions.

Getting started

Knowing what to do next is half the battle. School often assumes kids already have that muscle.

Keeping track

Materials, deadlines, and steps pile up. Without systems, even smart students look “unmotivated.”

Following through

Starting is not finishing. Kids need practice finishing hard things — with a coach, not another reminder app.

The school model

School, rebuilt around the student.

We kept the parts of high school that change a life and rebuilt the rest — AI for academics, coaches for the human work, pathways into the real world, and time outside.

Focused, personalized academics

AI tutors deliver core academics one-on-one, adapting to each student's pace and gaps — including credit recovery and acceleration when they're ready.

Human skills, taught on purpose

Getting started, staying organized, communicating, and persisting — practiced with a live coach until students can run their own systems.

Mentorship + real-world pathways

Every student has a dedicated coach who knows them and holds a high bar — plus hands-on pathways into design, tech, trades, business, and internships.

Outdoor experiential learning

The Wasatch is our second campus. Weekly expeditions, service, and hands-on challenges build resilience, teamwork, and a body that's as engaged as the mind.

A day at CMYK

Questions, answered

The things families ask first.

Why "CMYK"?

CMYK is the four inks — cyan, magenta, yellow, and black — that layer together to make every color you've ever seen. At CMYK High, those colors map to human skill families: Personal, Professional, Interpersonal, and Global. Students grow across all four, and the combination is what makes a full person — not a single track.

Who is CMYK High the right fit for?

Capable teens who've become bored, disengaged, or lost in a traditional classroom — especially kids who ask “Why am I doing this?” and who need help with the everyday skills school assumes but rarely teaches: getting started, keeping track of work and materials, managing time, and finishing what they start. It's also a fit for students recovering credits, ready to accelerate, or who simply learn differently.

What do you mean by human skills?

Human skills are the Personal, Professional, Interpersonal, and Global competencies that sit alongside academics — things like Self Management, Communication, Resilience, and contributing on a team. Coaches practice them with students in person through routines, plans, and real projects. We don't outsource that work to phone apps or reminder stacks.

Where is the school located?

CMYK High is based in Lehi, Utah, on the Wasatch Front — with the mountains, canyons, and rivers of northern Utah serving as our outdoor campus for weekly field days and expeditions.

What are pathways?

Pathways connect academics to real direction — design and digital media, technology and making, skilled trades and business — through hands-on projects, internships, and industry credentials. They help students answer “Why am I doing this?” with work that leads somewhere.

Will students learn to use AI themselves — not just learn from it?

Yes. There's a real difference between learning from AI and learning to use it, and we teach both. Alongside their AI tutor, students get hands-on practice using AI as a tool and thinking partner — how to prompt it well, pressure-test what it gives back, and apply it to writing, research, design, coding, and problem-solving.

Do the skill credentials replace grades or a transcript?

No. Transcripts still serve their purpose. Alongside them, students turn everyday coursework and projects into evidence of growth in human skills a GPA was never designed to show. Ratings like Emerging, Developed, and Solidified are earned through demonstrated evidence over time. Students own the credential; the system authenticates it. The credentials supplement the transcript — they don't replace it.

How big are the cohorts?

Small by design. We keep cohorts intentionally limited so every student is truly known by their coach. Our founding class is especially small — one reason to join the waitlist early.

What about accreditation, tuition, and a diploma?

We're finalizing these details as we prepare to open, and accreditation and a recognized path to graduation are core priorities. Waitlist families will be the first to receive full information on tuition, the academic pathway, and enrollment.
